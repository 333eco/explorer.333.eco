# Acceptable use

Draft of 25 November 2026.

This policy covers every service whose documents are listed at legal.333.eco. It says what no one may do on any of them. Each service's own terms apply too, and they may add rules of their own.

## What no one may do

- **Pretend to be someone else.** Do not impersonate a person, a business or one of these services, and do not take a name, a page or a tag to mislead people about who you are.
- **Harass anyone.** Do not threaten, stalk, bully or intimidate a person, or urge others to.
- **Use slurs.** Do not attack people for who they are: their race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, caste, sex, gender, sexual orientation, disability or age.
- **Post or send illegal content.** Nothing that is against the law, and never anything that sexualizes a child.
- **Use someone else's work without permission.** Do not upload, post or share a work protected by copyright unless it is yours or you have the right to.
- **Spam, or automate abuse.** Do not send bulk or unwanted messages, make names or accounts in bulk, or use scripts or agents to do anything this policy forbids.
- **Attack the services.** Do not probe, scan, overload or break into a service, get around its limits, or try to reach what is not yours. To report a weakness you found, follow the security policy at legal.333.eco/security.

## How to report something

- **A page on 333.eco** (an @name or a #tag page): use "Report this page" on it, or write to abuse@333.eco.
- **A file kept at storage.333.eco:** write to notice@333.eco with the B-Link or the page where you saw it.
- **Anything else:** write to the contact in that service's own terms. The operators page at legal.333.eco/operators lists each one.

Say what you saw, where you saw it (its address), and why it breaks this policy.

## What may happen

When something breaks this policy, the service where it happened may remove the content, or end a name or an account, as that service's own terms say. Those terms also say how you will hear about it.

## Changes

A change to this policy is a new version at its own address, timestamped like every other document here.
